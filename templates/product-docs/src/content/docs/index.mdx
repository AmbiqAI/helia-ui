## How this site is organized

- **Install** is setup only. Everything a reader does once.
- **How-to** is task-shaped. One page per job, each with a visible outcome.
- **Reference** is generated or exhaustive. Nothing here needs to be readable
  end to end.
- **Guides** is the long-form explanation: concepts, tradeoffs, background.
