Page layout is the consuming site's job. The package ships one layout part,
the carousel, because the scroll, snap, and tab behaviour is worth writing once
and the rail never needs to know what it is carrying.

## Showcase carousel

Use it for a larger peer set where showing every item at once would dominate
the page. Controls stay minimal and native horizontal scrolling stays
available.

The rail never sees its cards' data. `tabs` is one label per card in slot
order, and the script matches a tab to a card by position among the track's
children. Slot whatever card you like; the rail sizes it.

## The compact variant

`variant="compact"` is text-forward. The whole card is one bordered surface,
the media becomes a short band at the top, and cards drop to about a third of
the width so three read at once on a desktop viewport. Use it for update feeds
and other secondary sections.

## Sizing is the rail's

The track sets `--carousel-card-basis` and applies the flex basis and the
scroll-snap alignment to its own children, so a slotted card needs no carousel
knowledge at all. A card that wants a different width can still set its own
`flex` and win on specificity.
