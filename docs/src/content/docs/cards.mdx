## The card parts

`Card` is a `Surface` with no padding of its own. The padding belongs to the
parts, which is what lets `CardMedia` reach the edge of the same card. Every
part is optional and the order is yours: the card stacks what it is handed, and
wherever two padded parts meet, in either order, it drops the doubled gap. Set
`padding="none"` on a part and it owns its gaps from then on.

Write a title or a label on the same line as its tag, or wrap it in a `<span>`.
MDX reads children that start on a new line as flow content and wraps the text
in a paragraph; children on the opening line stay phrasing.

Nothing here knows what a product, a demo or a status is. The title, the
metadata and the actions are children, so a card never decides where it links.

The card itself is never an anchor. A whole-card link is `href` on `CardHeader`:
the title carries the link and the recipe stretches it over the card, which
leaves the actions and the metadata clickable in their own right.

### Without media

A header and a body, and a whole-card link instead of an action. `interactive`
adds the shared hover background, and the card takes the focus ring when the
stretched header link is focused.

<div className="helia-card-grid helia-equal-grid not-content">
  <Card interactive>
    <CardHeader titleAs="h4" href="#without-media">
      <Fragment slot="overline">Concept</Fragment>
      <span>Choose a deployment path</span>
    </CardHeader>
    <CardContent>
      When to compile ahead of time and when to stay on the runtime.
    </CardContent>
  </Card>
  <Card tone="muted">
    <CardHeader titleAs="h4">
      <Fragment slot="meta">
        <Badge tone="warning" dot>
          <span>Check first</span>
        </Badge>
      </Fragment>
      <span>Supported hardware</span>
    </CardHeader>
    <CardContent>
      A muted card recedes when it sits inside another surface.
    </CardContent>
  </Card>
</div>

### Actions only

`CardActions` is a flex row around whatever you pass it. `align` is `start`,
`end`, or `between`.

<div className="helia-card-grid helia-equal-grid not-content">
  <Card>
    <CardActions>
      <Button href="#actions-only" size="sm">
        <span>Start</span>
      </Button>
      <Button href="#actions-only" size="sm" variant="secondary">
        <span>Browse</span>
      </Button>
    </CardActions>
  </Card>
  <Card>
    <CardActions align="end">
      <Chip href="#actions-only">Docs</Chip>
      <Chip href="#actions-only">Source</Chip>
    </CardActions>
  </Card>
</div>

### Media ratios

`CardMedia` owns the frame; the media it receives fills it. `fit="contain"`
keeps a logo or a diagram whole instead of cropping it.

<div className="helia-card-grid helia-equal-grid not-content">
  <Card>
    <CardMedia ratio="1/1">
      <Media treatment="slate" />
    </CardMedia>
    <CardContent>1/1</CardContent>
  </Card>
  <Card>
    <CardMedia ratio="4/3">
      <Media treatment="graphite" />
    </CardMedia>
    <CardContent>4/3</CardContent>
  </Card>
  <Card>
    <CardMedia ratio="16/9">
      <Media treatment="wire" />
    </CardMedia>
    <CardContent>16/9</CardContent>
  </Card>
</div>

## Badge

Six tones and an optional leading dot. The tones are semantic, not a status
vocabulary: map your own statuses onto them in the composition that knows what
they mean.

The label is required: the dot is decorative and hidden from assistive
technology, so a badge with no label renders nothing.

<div className="helia-badge-row">
  <Badge>Neutral</Badge>
  <Badge tone="info">Info</Badge>
  <Badge tone="success">Success</Badge>
  <Badge tone="warning">Warning</Badge>
  <Badge tone="danger">Danger</Badge>
  <Badge tone="accent">Accent</Badge>
</div>

<div className="helia-badge-row">
  <Badge dot>Neutral</Badge>
  <Badge tone="success" dot>
    <span>Success</span>
  </Badge>
  <Badge tone="danger" dot>
    <span>Danger</span>
  </Badge>
  <Badge size="md" tone="info">
    <span>Medium</span>
  </Badge>
  <Badge size="md" tone="warning" dot>
    <span>Medium with dot</span>
  </Badge>
</div>

The artwork in these examples comes from `Media`, which has a page of its
own under [Media](/helia-ui/media/).
